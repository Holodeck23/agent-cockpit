Cockpit · a new look · Lumen

A quiet room where the only light is your agents.

The surfaces are ink and paper, and they hold still. Your agents are the light in the room: points that breathe while they think and circle while they work. When one needs you, it glows like a candle, never like an alarm.

Calm, because nothing moves unless an agent is working. Organized, because every surface has one job. Alive, because work shows up as light. And a little magical.

How things move

Motion shows only state. Nothing loops unless an agent is working, everything eases in and out, and with Reduce Motion on, all of it holds still.

The orbit

Replaces the three bouncing bars, everywhere they appeared: tabs, filters, status, steps.

The working light

Replaces the KITT scanner. One soft light drifts along the message box and the edge glows faintly. When the agent is idle, nothing moves.

Colour

Moonpaper by day, night by night. Colour has three jobs and no others: lumen (violet) means an agent is working, candle (gold) means it needs you, tide (teal) means you picked it. Green appears only as a small done tick and rose only as a small error mark. Primary buttons are ink.

The rules

Adopted from the Heartbeat concept (2026-10-10), applied to Lumen. Every screen follows them.

Colour has three jobsWorking is lumen, needs you is candle gold, selected is tide. Nothing else gets colour. Project colours live only on the small planet avatars.
One glow at a timeWhile the working light is lit, or a decision is open, nothing else on screen blooms. List rows hold still.
Status is a lightNever a filled pill. Working is the orbit, needs you is a gold candle, ready is quiet text, error is one rose mark.
No bubblesTurns are a log with hairlines. Your words are a short line with a rule at the edge; the agent's reply reads as a page.
Nothing moves unless an agent worksMotion only shows state. Theme changes land at once. With Reduce Motion on, everything holds still.
Ink, not colour, for actionsSend, New and primary buttons are ink and lift a little on hover.

What changes

Everything you see changes. Everything that works stays as it is.

Logo & app iconThe orbit mark replaces the blue bar-chart tile, in the app, the .icns, the PWA icons and the DMG background.
Dock animationThe moon orbits the light. It replaces the rising bars, with one look for light and dark.
KITT scanner → working light146 lamps become one soft light. It's calmer and much lighter for the page.
Every screenTokens, chrome, list, conversation, composer, menus, modals, first run, files, workflows and phone.
Landing siteThe Vercel page moves to the same night-and-paper look, after the app.
Name: kept for nowStill "Cockpit" everywhere. A rename touches install, updates, storage and the agents' MCP tools, so it's a separate decision.
